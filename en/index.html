What people ask us before they start

Is it safe?

Before you start we go over your health with you. Your trainer raises the intensity little by little and sets it area by area for the whole session. It is not suitable with a pacemaker, during pregnancy or with epilepsy: tell us about your case.

Does it hurt?

You feel a tingling and your muscles working. If anything bothers you, your trainer turns the intensity down straight away.

Will I be sore?

Some soreness in the first days is normal, as with any new training.

I have a delicate back. Can I do Pilates?

Tell us before you start. With 4 people at most, the trainer adapts every exercise to you.

What should I wear?

Comfortable sportswear and a bottle of water. For EMS we tell you what to wear under the vest when you book.
